Design rules

Four rules every screen follows

Rule 1

Rank by deadline, not risk score

A weekly risk score was computed before Harold's discharge. Fresh events and deadlines decide the order.

Rule 2

Show conflicts, never resolve them silently

When the discharge list and the pharmacy disagree, Maria sees both, side by side.

Rule 3

Every fact has a source and an age

"Pharmacy · 2 days" and "Claims · 52 days" look different, so stale data is never mistaken for news.

Rule 4

AI drafts, Maria decides

Nothing is sent, changed or filed without her confirmation and signature.
